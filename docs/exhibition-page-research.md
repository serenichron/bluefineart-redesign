# Exhibition page research (2026-11-06)

Fetched live: David Zwirner, Pace, Lisson, White Cube, Victoria Miro, Kathryn Markel, Art Basel 2026 collector report.
Blocked (from memory only): Artsy, Gagosian, Hauser & Wirth, Perrotin.

## What a visitor wants, in order
1. **Hero**: one big work or room shot, with artist, title, dates. The art sells the visit first.
2. **Key facts**: dates, opening night, address, hours, "Book a viewing". First question is "when and where".
3. **Enquire button in the hero** for collectors who already know (Zwirner, Lisson).
4. **Short intro** (3-5 lines) + "Read more" for the full essay (Lisson, White Cube, Pace).
5. **The works, one by one**: image, full caption, price or "Price on request", Enquire per work (Victoria Miro).
6. **Installation views**: show scale and the real room (Zwirner, Victoria Miro).
7. **Video** (White Cube "In the Studio").
8. **About the artist**: short bio + link (White Cube, Pace, Lisson).
9. **Downloads**: press release and list of works PDF (Victoria Miro, Lisson).
10. **Events**: opening RSVP, talks. Zwirner lists events but has no RSVP.
11. **Visit block**: map, hours, phone, WhatsApp.
12. **Related exhibitions + newsletter.**

## Patterns
- Sticky in-page nav: Overview / Works / Installation / About (Zwirner, Pace).
- Full caption: artist, *title*, year, medium, size in in and cm (Victoria Miro).
- Visible prices sell better: Artsy says 5-6x more likely to sell; 62% of collectors dislike hidden prices. Platform claims, not proof.
- White page, one restrained type family, colour comes from the art.
- Online sales were ~15% of the market in 2025. The page's job is to start a conversation or a visit, not a checkout.
- Mobile: single column, sticky Enquire + WhatsApp bar, pinch-zoom, caption under image, tap-to-call and tap-to-map.
- Enquiry form: artwork prefilled, name + email required, phone optional.
- WhatsApp: no top gallery offers it. A real edge for Blue Gallery.
- "View in room" scale preview: Artsy has it, none of the big galleries do.

## Where Artsy falls short
- Related works from other galleries pull buyers away.
- Enquiries stay inside Artsy, often need a login. No WhatsApp, call or booking.
- Search traffic goes to artsy.net, not bluefineart.com.
- Same template for every show. No brand, no pacing.
- Physical show details are thin: hours, map, RSVP.
- No PDFs, no video, essay cut short.
- No gallery newsletter sign-up.
