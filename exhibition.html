About the artist

Isaac Maimon

Isaac Maimon is an Israeli painter, known for his lyrical style and his scenes of elegant urban life. He was born in 1951 to a French-speaking family in Israel.

He trained at the Avni Institute of Fine Arts in Tel Aviv. The café culture of Paris, and painters like Toulouse-Lautrec and Matisse, shaped his voice. In the 1980s he taught at art schools across Israel.

His work has been shown in the United States, Europe and Israel. It is held in private collections around the world.
