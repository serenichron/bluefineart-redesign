About us

Sophisticated flair,
contemporary art.

Blue Gallery shows original collections by internationally acclaimed artists. Landscapes, abstracts, semi-realism, realism and figurative work.

Founder and owner Rami Rotkopf grew up in his family's art business in Tel Aviv, Israel. He knows the buzz of the art world well, and he brings that energy to Blue Gallery.

He has spent a lifetime in galleries. Today he finds and shows the work of gifted artists, and represents some of the best-known contemporary artists working now.
